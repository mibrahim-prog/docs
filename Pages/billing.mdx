**Billing & Subscription** shows your account balance, subscriptions, usage, and invoices. Only **Admins** can access this page.

**To open it:** in the sidebar, under **Configuration**, click **Billing**.

## Current balance

The balance card at the top shows:

| Item | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Current Balance** | Funds available, with a **Credit Available** badge when credit applies. |
| **Spent this month** | Usage so far in the current month. |
| **Plan type** | For example **Postpaid**. |
| **Remaining / Used** | A progress bar comparing remaining and used balance. |

Call scoring, custom parameters, trackers, stereo accuracy, and agent reports are charged against this balance. Your balance is also shown on the [Upload Calls](/Pages/callUpload) page.

## Subscriptions

The **AI Assistant** subscription unlocks the _Talk to Your Data_ chat assistant (see [AI Assistant](/Pages/aiAssistant)). Its card shows the price, its status (for example _Active until …_), and an **Auto recharge** toggle that renews it automatically. Click **Cancel Subscription** to stop it.

## Usage and totals

| Card | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Paid** | Everything paid since the account was created. |
| **Consumed Balance** | Everything used to date. |
| **Current Month Consumption** | A daily breakdown of this month's usage. |

## Invoices and payment history

- **Invoices** lists your monthly billing statements. Click **PDF** to download one, or **Export** to download the list. Months without an invoice show _N/A_.
- **Payment History** lists every transaction on record.

## What is charged

| Item | Basis |
| --- | --- |
| Call scoring | Per call minute |
| Custom parameters | Small extra charge per call minute, shown on each parameter |
| Trackers | Extra charge per call minute while switched on |
| Stereo accuracy | Higher per-call cost for stereo processing |
| Agent Reports | \$1.00 per agent per month when first generated |
| Fatal incident conditions | Free |
| Daily call reports and fatal alerts by email | Free |

<Tip>
  **To control spend:** use the Integrator's **Daily Percentage** and **Daily Limit**, switch off trackers you don't need, and assign custom parameters only to the departments that use them.
</Tip>
