Welcome to the **Xperia User Guide**. This guide explains every screen in the Xperia portal — what it does, what each option means, and how to complete common tasks step by step.

## What is Xperia?

Xperia is an AI-powered call quality assurance (QA) platform. Instead of manually reviewing a small sample of calls, you upload recordings — or connect your call system — and Xperia scores **every** call against your QA scorecard. Each call receives:

- A **score out of 100**, broken down by category and parameter
- An AI-written **summary** of the conversation
- The **reasoning** behind every score
- Practical **coaching suggestions** for the agent
- Flags for **fatal incidents** and business signals such as churn risk

## How Xperia works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Bring calls in">
    Upload recordings manually from [Upload Calls](/Pages/callUpload), or connect your phone system with the [Integrator](/Pages/integrations) so calls arrive automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Xperia scores every call">
    Each call is transcribed and evaluated against your scorecard: standard **categories**, your own **custom parameters**, **fatal incident** conditions, and **trackers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the results">
    Open any call in [Call Scoring](/Pages/callScoring) to see the full report, adjust scores if needed, and mark it as reviewed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Act on the insights">
    Track performance on the [Dashboard](/Pages/dashboard), send monthly [Agent Reports](/Pages/agentReports), receive instant [fatal incident alerts](/Pages/Fatal_Alerts), and ask questions with the [AI Assistant](/Pages/aiAssistant).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key terms

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Category" icon="layer-group">
    A built-in scoring area — Call Opening, Closing, Communication, Hold Procedure, Listening, Personalisation, Probing, Responsibility, Tone, or Upselling. Categories share the 100-point total.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Custom parameter" icon="sliders">
    A check you define in plain language (for example, _agent did not verify the customer's identity_). It is added to the scorecard and affects the score.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Fatal incident" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    A serious breach, such as abusive language or a missed compliance disclosure. A fatal incident voids the call and scores it **zero**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Tracker" icon="satellite-dish">
    A specialized parameter, such as Churn Risk, that is watched on every call for insight only. Trackers **do not** affect the score.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Department" icon="building">
    A team such as Inbound or Sales. Each department can have its own parameters and weightage.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Work code" icon="hashtag">
    A short label describing what a call was about, for example _order delivery issue_.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Call type" icon="phone">
    Xperia handles **Human** agent calls and **AI** agent calls separately. Most screens include a Call Type filter.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
