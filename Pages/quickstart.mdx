# Quickstart

Get started with Xperia and analyze your first call in a few simple steps.

In this guide, you'll learn how to sign in, upload a call recording,
wait for AI processing, and review the call score and analysis.

## Before you start

Make sure you have:

- An active Xperia account
- Access to your Xperia organization
- A supported call recording
- The required user permissions to upload and review calls

## Step 1: Sign in to Xperia

Open the Xperia application and sign in using your account credentials.

After signing in, you'll be taken to the Xperia dashboard where you can
access call scoring, analytics, user management, and other available features.

## Step 2: Upload a call recording

1. Open **Call Scoring** from the navigation menu.
2. Select **Upload Call**.
3. Select the call recording you want to analyze.
4. Select the required **Agent**.
5. Submit the call for processing.

Xperia sends the recording for AI-powered analysis.

## Step 3: Wait for AI processing

After submitting the recording, Xperia processes the call and evaluates it
against the configured quality parameters.

The call initially appears with a **Processing** status.

Once processing is complete, the status changes to **Completed**.

## Step 4: Review the call

Open the completed call to review its analysis.

Depending on your configuration, you can review:

- Agent name
- Recording date and time
- Call duration
- Obtained score
- Total score
- Call status
- Quality parameters
- AI-generated insights and feedback

## Step 5: Explore your analytics

After analyzing calls, open the **Analytics Dashboard** to monitor
performance and identify trends.

You can use analytics to understand:

- Agent performance
- Call quality
- Performance trends
- Key quality metrics
