# Xperia Documentation

## AI-Powered Call Quality Assurance

Xperia is an AI-powered call quality assurance platform that helps contact centers
analyze, score, and improve customer conversations at scale.

Instead of manually reviewing a small sample of calls, Xperia analyzes calls against
configurable quality criteria and provides detailed scores, insights, summaries,
and actionable feedback.

### What you can do with Xperia

- **Analyze calls** — Upload recordings or integrate Xperia with your existing
  telephony systems.
- **Score agents automatically** — Evaluate calls against configurable quality
  parameters and scorecards.
- **Monitor performance** — Track agent performance, call quality, trends,
  and key metrics through analytics dashboards.
- **Create custom parameters** — Define your own quality requirements for
  specific business processes or teams.
- **Review call results** — View scores, summaries, feedback, and detailed
  call-level evaluations.
- **Manage access** — Control what users can access through role-based
  permissions.
- **Analyze multilingual calls** — Process conversations across multiple
  languages, including conversations that switch languages.
- **Integrate with your systems** — Connect Xperia with telephony and
  communication platforms to automate call ingestion.

Xperia is designed for QA teams, supervisors, contact-center managers, and
organizations that want to improve agent performance and customer service
using AI-driven call analysis.
