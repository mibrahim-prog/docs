# Fatal Alerts

## What are Fatal Alerts?

Fatal Alerts help **supervisors stay informed about fatal calls** by sending an email notification when a call is identified and scored as a **fatal incident**.

The notification contains the call's **report and summary**, allowing supervisors to review the incident and take the necessary action.

## How to Configure Fatal Alerts

<Steps>

  <Step title="Open Mail Config">

    Open the **Mail Config** page from the menu.

    ![Mail Config page](/Images/FatalAlerts/Mail_Config.png)

  </Step>

  <Step title="Open Fatal Alerts">

    On the **Reports Subscriptions** page, click the **Fatal Alerts** tab.

    ![Fatal Alerts tab](/Images/FatalAlerts/Mail_Config_FA.png)

  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable Fatal Incident Alerts">

    Turn on the **Fatal Incident Alerts** toggle.

    Once enabled, Xperia can send email notifications when a call is identified as a fatal incident.

    ![Enable Fatal Alerts](/Images/FatalAlerts/Mail_Config_togglebtn.png)

  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Recipient Email">

    Under **Recipients (To)**, enter the email address that should receive fatal incident notifications.

    Click the **+** button to add the email address.

    You can add multiple recipient email addresses if required.

    ![Add recipient email](/Images/FatalAlerts/Mail_Config_recipientemail.png)

  </Step>

  <Step title="Add CC Recipients">

    If another person should receive a copy of the notification, enter their email address under **CC Recipients**.

    Click the **+** button to add the email address.

    **CC Recipients are optional.**

    ![Add CC recipient](/Images/FatalAlerts/Mail_Config_ccrecipient.png)

  </Step>

  <Step title="Save Your Changes">

    Click **Save changes** to save the Fatal Alerts configuration.

    ![Save Fatal Alerts](/Images/FatalAlerts/Mail_Config_savebtn.png)

  </Step>

</Steps>

## How It Works

Once Fatal Alerts are configured, Xperia automatically monitors the results of uploaded calls.

When a call is identified as a **fatal incident**, Xperia sends an email notification to the configured recipients.

The notification process is:

**Call Uploaded → Call Analyzed → Fatal Incident Detected → Email Alert Sent → Supervisor Reviews Call**

The supervisor can then review the **call report and summary** included in the notification.

> **Note:** Fatal Alert emails are sent only when Fatal Alerts are enabled and at least one recipient email address has been configured and saved.

## Recipient Settings

| Setting | Description |
|---|---|
| **Fatal Incident Alerts** | Enables or disables fatal incident email notifications. |
| **Recipients (To)** | Email addresses that receive the fatal incident notification. |
| **CC Recipients** | Optional email addresses that receive a copy of the notification. |
| **+** | Adds the entered email address to the recipient list. |
| **Save changes** | Saves the Fatal Alerts configuration. |

> **Note:** If no recipient email address is configured, no Fatal Alert email will be sent.
