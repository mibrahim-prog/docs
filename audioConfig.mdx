# Audio Configuration

## What is Audio Configuration?

Audio Configuration helps Xperia know **which voice is the agent** and **which voice is the customer** in a call.

You can choose if the agent's voice is on the **Left channel** or **Right channel**.

## How to Configure Audio

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Audio Config">
    Open the **Audio Config** page from the menu.

    ![Audio Config page](/Images/Click_AudioConfig.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Edit Setting">
    Click the **Edit Setting** button.

    ![Edit Setting button](/Images/Click_EditButton.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn On the Toggle">
    Turn on the **Dual-channel (stereo) recordings** toggle.

    ![Dual-channel toggle](/Images/Click_toggle.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the Agent Channel">
    Select either the **Left channel** or **Right channel** for the agent.

    ![Choose agent channel](/Images/Select_LCRC.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Your Changes">
    Click **Save Changes** to save your settings.

    ![Save Changes button](/Images/Click_SaveButton.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How It Works

After you save the settings and upload a new call:

- The channel you selected is the **agent's voice**.
- The other channel is the **customer's voice**.

For example:

**Agent → Left channel**<br />**Customer → Right channel**

or

**Agent → Right channel**<br />**Customer → Left channel**

> **Note:** These settings apply to new calls. Existing call scores are not changed.
